Privacy and Cookies Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 · Version 1

This policy explains how the Turbo IMAP website handles information. It covers the pages hosted at emileite.github.io only. The Turbo IMAP desktop application is separate: email data and credentials are processed and stored locally on your computer, not sent to this website.

1. Who we are

Turbo IMAP is an open-source project maintained at github.com/emileite/turbo-imap . The website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. You can contact the project by opening an issue in the repository. We do not require an account, login, newsletter subscription or contact form to use the website.

2. What the website uses

We do not sell personal data. The website uses the following services and storage:

Category What it does When it is used Provider
Essential storage Stores your cookie choice in your browser using the local storage key turboimap-consent for up to 180 days. Always, to remember your choice. This website (your browser).
Analytics Google Analytics 4 measures page views and general traffic statistics, such as approximate location, device, browser and referring page. Only after you accept non-essential cookies. Google.
Advertising Google AdSense may set cookies and use device information to show and measure ads. Personalized advertising depends on your consent and Google settings. Only after you accept non-essential cookies. Google.
GitHub data The home page requests public repository and release information from the GitHub API to display stars, forks and the latest release. When the home page loads. GitHub.

3. Cookies and local storage

The consent choice itself is stored in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. If you accept, Google Analytics and Google AdSense may set cookies such as _ga, _ga_* and advertising cookies. The exact cookie names and lifetimes are controlled by Google and may change.

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. Doing so will make the consent banner appear again.

4. Your choices

When you first visit a page that uses analytics or advertising, a banner lets you accept all or reject non-essential services. If you reject, the Google Analytics and Google AdSense scripts are not loaded on that page.

You can change your choice later with the Cookie settings link in the footer. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Privacy Policy — covers Google Analytics and Google AdSense.
  • How Google uses information for advertising .
  • GitHub Privacy Statement — covers requests to the GitHub API and GitHub Pages hosting.

6. Data retention

Your consent choice is kept for up to 180 days. Analytics and advertising data are retained by Google according to its own settings and policies. We do not store the GitHub API data on a server; it is displayed in your browser.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because this website does not have user accounts, most requests can be handled directly in your browser. For questions or requests, open an issue at github.com/emileite/turbo-imap/issues . You may also contact your local data protection authority.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the services it uses change. The version and date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Turbo IMAP repository .
